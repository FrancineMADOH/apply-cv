Business Analyst — Digital Transformation
Paris, Île-de-France — Hybrid

About the role

We are looking for a Business Analyst to join our digital transformation team. You will work closely with business stakeholders and IT teams to define, document, and deliver process improvements across our operations.

Responsibilities

- Gather and analyse business requirements through workshops and interviews
- Write functional specifications (user stories, use cases, acceptance criteria)
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies and maintain the product backlog
- Coordinate with development teams and ensure delivery quality
- Produce clear documentation and project status reports

Required profile

- 3+ years of experience as a Business Analyst or similar role
- Solid knowledge of Agile / Scrum methodology
- Experience with JIRA and Confluence
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Ability to work with both technical and non-technical stakeholders

Nice to have

- Experience in the insurance or banking sector
- Knowledge of BPMN process modelling

Contract: CDI (permanent)
Salary: 40–50K€ depending on experience
Start date: ASAP
